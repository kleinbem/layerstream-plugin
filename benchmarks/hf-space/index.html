Qwen2.5-14B Iterative Optimization (R2 → R8)

Every engineering change profiled with microsecond I/O + DMA timers on bare-metal Jetson Orin Nano (`code_edit` decode tok/s).

Takeaway: Zero-copy O_DIRECT → cudaHostAlloc + per-tensor cudaMemcpyAsync + io_threads=1 + adaptive speculation lifted 14B from 0.19 tok/s (pure stream) to 3.41–5.97 tok/s with 2,961 MiB/s sustained through LUKS2 dm-crypt.

Sparse MoE + Negative Ablations

Honest negative ablations matter just as much as wins — here is what actually hurt vs. helped on real hardware.

Key findings: (1) Multi-threaded O_DIRECT (io_threads=4) regressed -57% on Linux LUKS2 dm-crypt. (2) Spending 580 MiB on a 1.5B draft instead of extra resident layers lost 2.5×–3.0× to 0.5B. (3) TinyLFU hot-expert caching jumped OLMoE 15.8×–17.9× (`0.76 → 13.67 tok/s`).
